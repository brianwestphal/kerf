Eleven runnable showcase apps. Each has its own page, an animated preview, and a one-click **Run live** below. They live under [`site/src/examples/complete/`](https://github.com/brianwestphal/kerf/tree/main/site/src/examples/complete) as real projects (drop into a fresh project with `kerfjs` installed and they run — ten are Vite projects; the live poll is served as authored source with no build at all). The htmx cart island and the counter store are also the worked examples in the [htmx](/kerf/migrating/htmx/) and [Redux](/kerf/migrating/redux/) migration guides.

The thumbnails are animated captures of the real apps running — click a card to open its page, or **Run live** to open the running demo. (Captured with [`domotion-svg`](https://github.com/brianwestphal/domotion); they animate inside the page and scale crisply.)
