## Interpretation

The pre-guidance commits directly support the component-selection, CSS-restraint, spacing-ownership, negative-space, and neighboring-screen advice added in KF-TMA3JT. Later commits show teams continuing to make and verify those choices in real applications, while HS2-JJ6ZE1 and HS2-FC8HEF show that layout errors and component gaps remain. The short post-guidance window, pre-existing work in progress, and absence of a record that either agent read the new guidance prevent a causal improvement claim. The v3 AI regression baseline remains unmeasured; its isolated pilot and harness repairs are recorded on KF-YDXK44, KF-6CX7J5, KF-PM0B43, and KF-NSKY53.
