A free-text field with a filtered suggestion list that can also create a new value. Let people pick an existing value or create a new one by typing (tags, labels, recipients), with the themed field chrome, the ARIA combobox pattern, and a touch-safe font size, without a native datalist or app CSS. Wiring: wireComboboxes from @kerfjs/ui/wire-comboboxes (required). The app owns the suggestions and committed values, validating created values, and clearing the field after commit.
